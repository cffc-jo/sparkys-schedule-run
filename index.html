HOW TO PLAY

GOAL

Run the full season and reveal every opponent in the fastest time.

CONTROLS

PHONE

  • TAP or SWIPE UP to jump
  • TAP AGAIN in the air to double-jump
  • SWIPE DOWN to duck

KEYBOARD

  • SPACE W ↑ jump
    (again in the air to double-jump)
  • S ↓ duck
  • P ESC pause

KEY

  • Soccer ball = seconds of invincibility
  • Hitting a box = + second penalty
  • Clearing a box = a small speed boost

You always finish the run. Penalties only add time.
